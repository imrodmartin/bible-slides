Paul leaves Titus on Crete to finish organizing young churches surrounded by empty talkers and lazy liars. His prescription never wavers: sound doctrine that shows up in changed lives and good works.

Summary
Three movements through Titus
Ch 1
Sound doctrine & order

Appoint qualified elders and silence the empty talkers. Truth must be guarded and led.

Ch 2
Grace that trains

Grace has appeared — teaching every believer to live godly while we await the blessed hope.

Ch 3
Saved for good works

Washed and renewed by the Spirit, justified by grace, devoted to doing what is good.

Discussion
For your group

Questions worth sitting with.

1

Titus ties sound doctrine to changed lives. Where might your beliefs and your behavior be telling two different stories?

2

Grace "teaches us to say No" (2:12). What is one ungodly habit grace is currently training you to refuse?

3

Paul roots it all in mercy, not merit (3:5). How does resting in that mercy free you to do more good, not less?

4

We wait for "the blessed hope—the appearing" (2:13). How would living as if Christ could appear today reshape your week?
